“Lenox blue”

You might have noticed that our logo and website feature a striking shade of blue. As much as we like it, this isn't just an aesthetic choice: it expresses a vital part of our mission. So much of the discourse around technology feels chaotic, rapid-fire, and “hot.” This is due to the attention economy: even when creators would prefer to distance themselves from sensationalism and clickbait, they’re forced to compete with an ever-growing number of claims on our mental bandwidth. In this context, nuanced and thoughtful scholarship rarely rises to the surface, and it’s tempting to opt out of learning about science and tech entirely. 

Since Lenox's mission isn't about content, we don’t face this challenge. Our goal is to bring people together in real time to confront topics we mostly encounter in the solitary and sped-up world of the internet. In doing so, we aim to “cool down” knowledge that's essential for understanding technoscientific change, but that can feel like a burden in the midst of so many attention traps. Hence blue: the color of cool and calm.
