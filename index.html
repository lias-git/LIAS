THE LENOX INSTITUTE
FOR ADVANCED STUDY

Bridging deep knowledge and collective inquiry
in a rapidly changing world.

The rapid pace of scientific and technological change is increasingly challenging to navigate. Making sense of a world driven by innovation means continuous self-education. Yet most adult learning takes place in isolation, without mentors, friends, and spaces for spontaneous interaction. When learning takes place outside of social environments, it’s less likely to yield deep insight and inspire positive social action.

The Lenox Institute for Advanced Study brings affordable, world-class courses on the social impact of science and technology to adult learners of all backgrounds. We operate online and at select locations in New York City.
