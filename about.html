About

The Lenox Institute for Advanced Study offers college-level courses, events, and workshops that explore the social implications of scientific and technological development. Operating beyond traditional academic settings, we respond to the educational needs of the twenty-first century, where the rapid pace of innovation makes critical science and tech studies more urgent than ever.

Our core value is collaboration. In an age where adult learning often takes place in individually-focused digital spaces, we serve as a meaningful alternative, treating community bonds as essential to our pedagogical approach. This emphasis reflects our view of teaching and learning as fundamentally pro-social activities, along with our commitment to addressing the everyday challenges of technological life.

We are currently in our launch phase. If you’d like to join us, please follow us on Instagram and LinkedIn and sign up for our newsletter.
